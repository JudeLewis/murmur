# Wispr Flow features, 2 November 2026

Jude asked for Wispr Flow's best ideas, then picked all six, plus a friendlier keyboard and a way to turn the microphone off.

- Snippets: say a phrase, get saved text (Mac and iPhone).
- Spoken commands: "new line", "new paragraph", and "press enter" at the end.
- Word list and snippets screen shared by the Mac window and the iPhone app, with "Add a fix from a recent dictation".
- Casual style: per app on the Mac (Messages and WhatsApp to start), the "Aa" switch on the iPhone keyboard.
- Hold Fn to talk (Mac), as well as double tap.
- Music turned down while listening (Mac), put back even after a quit or crash.
- The iPhone keyboard redone in Wispr Flow's style: top bar with mic, moving bars, ✕ and ✓; number and punctuation keys; undo.
- A "turn mic off" button on the keyboard, after Jude asked whether Murmur was still listening (the orange dot).
- The model wait after each iPhone update: a "Getting Murmur ready" card with a timer, screen kept awake, install retries opening the app. Keeping the same build number was tested and doesn't avoid the wait.
- Code review found 15 problems, all fixed, including word list removals being lost and "new line" firing mid-sentence. 112 tests.
