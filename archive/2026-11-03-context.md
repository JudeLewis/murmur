# Knowing what you're writing, 3 November 2026

Jude asked for emails to be laid out as emails, for commands mid-dictation, and for Murmur to ignore talk to someone in the room. Jude: "looks and feels good".

- Emails (Mac): in a mail app or webmail, the greeting and the sign-off get their own lines, by fixed rules that never change the words.
- "Scratch that" takes out the sentence before it, with or without the punctuation the model sometimes leaves off.
- A single Fn tap pauses while listening; nothing said meanwhile is kept.
- Quieter voices left out: sentences under 0.3 of your loudness. Tested with a second voice: left out at a third of the loudness or less, kept at 60%.
- Apple Intelligence deciding what's side chat was tested on 10 dictations, twice each: 11 of 20 right, and it changed real text in 3 of 10 clean ones. Not built.
- Code review found 10 problems, all fixed. 185 tests.
