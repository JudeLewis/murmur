# Mac extras, the same on iPhone, and Type as I speak, 2 to 3 November 2026

Jude asked what Wispr Flow's computer version does, picked all five ideas, then asked for them on the iPhone and for the words to appear while talking.

- Command Mode (Mac): hold Fn and Control, say what to do with the selected text; Apple Intelligence on the Mac rewrites it, or writes new text.
- Fixing "no wait, I mean" with Apple Intelligence, only ever taking words out.
- Paste the last dictation again: Control-Command-V.
- Whisper mode: quiet recordings made up to 20 times louder; off to start with.
- Choosing the microphone, with Automatic using another mic when the lid is shut.
- On iPhone too: the "no wait" fix and Whisper mode in the app's Settings, an "again" key, and ✨ Command Mode on the keyboard. Apple Intelligence answered with the app in the background every time Jude tried. Jude: "looks good for phone".
- Type as I speak (Mac): the recording is read again each second and words are typed once two readings agree, a second or two behind. Both test recordings came out exactly as reading them all at once. Jude: "looks good".
- A plain-English guide, "How Murmur works on your Mac", as a Claude Doc with a flow diagram.
- Code reviews found 15, 9 and 10 problems, all fixed; among them, the AI time limit that didn't end the wait, an AI answer landing where the cursor had moved, and Escape still typing a reading under way. 160 tests.
