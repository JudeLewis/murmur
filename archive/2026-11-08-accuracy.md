# Accuracy fixes, 8 Nov 2026

Jude reported that "question mark" came out as words, words were misheard, and full stops landed mid-sentence.

- Spoken punctuation: question mark, exclamation mark or point, full stop, comma, colon, semicolon, ellipsis, dot dot dot (SPEC.md 4.6). Mac and iPhone.
- Full stops that can't be real are removed (SPEC.md 4.3). Mac and iPhone.
- Type as I speak reads the whole recording each second and corrects what's typed, stopping corrections if you click or type (SPEC.md 2.9). Jude chose this over turning it off. Mac only, as the iPhone keyboard doesn't type as you speak.

Measured on test recordings in Mac voices. Jude's own voice still to be judged in use; if words are still misheard, TODO.md has the next step.
