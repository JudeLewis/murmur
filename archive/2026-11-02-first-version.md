# First version, 2 November 2026

Built in one session, started from the Clocked In folder and moved here.

- Double tap Fn to start and stop (Jude's choice over holding the key).
- Speech to text on the Mac with Parakeet Bark 3.0 Clean; Bark 3.0 word for word as the alternative.
- British spelling, word list, first-letter capital, trailing space.
- Paste with Command-V and restore the clipboard; recent dictations in the menu.
- The pill, the menu, sounds, Escape to cancel, 15-minute limit, retry a failed recording.
- Permission checks for microphone, Accessibility and the Fn key setting; Open at Login on first run.
- Install script, speech check script, 30 unit tests, pre-commit hook, CI.
